Stop Chasing Clients over Email for Documents & Answers

Chasing clients, contractors, and external vendors for files and missing information is one of the biggest time sinks in project delivery. Collectly 3.0 transforms document requests into interactive client forms linked directly to Jira work items — showing your team in real time what has arrived, what is approved, and what is still missing.

📝

1. Build a client form in minutes

Start from a template or build your own: file uploads, text, numbers, dates, yes/no and dropdowns, required or optional. Send one secure link; clients need no Atlassian account or license.

📥

2. Answers and files land in Jira

Clients fill in the form on any device and upload files up to 1 GB. Answers appear on the work item; files go to Jira, Collectly storage, or your own S3, Azure or Google Cloud bucket.

🔄

3. Review every answer, chase the rest

Approve or reject each answer with a comment, and the client fixes only that part. Email reminders before the due date, JQL statuses, and Jira Automation do the follow-up.

Deep-Dive Features & Capabilities

Seven Rich Field Types

Build comprehensive forms with file uploads, short text, long text, numbers, dates, yes/no toggles, and dropdown selectors. Mark fields as required or optional with custom hint descriptions.

Industry Templates & Project Presets

Launch instantly with built-in templates tailored for accounting, lending, legal, and HR onboarding workflows, or save your own reusable project templates.

Item-by-Item Review & Targeted Fixes

Review submissions right inside Jira. Approve valid answers or reject incomplete ones with feedback notes. Clients only re-submit the specific rejected items — no re-doing whole forms.

Automated Email Reminders

Set a due date and let Collectly handle chasing. Automated reminder emails go out 3 days and 1 day before the deadline, keeping client submissions on schedule without manual follow-ups.

Flexible Storage Choice (BYO Cloud Storage)

Choose where uploaded files live: directly as Jira issue attachments, in managed Collectly storage, or connected directly to your company's own AWS S3, Azure Blob, or Google Cloud Storage bucket.

Custom Email Delivery Providers

Send notification and reminder emails via Collectly's default service, or connect your own transactional provider account (Resend, SendGrid, or Mailgun) for total domain reputation control.

Dedicated Project Page & JQL Support

Monitor every active document request across the entire project on a centralized page. Filter and query request states using custom JQL statuses and trigger Jira Automation workflows.

Zero Client Login & High-Volume Uploads

External contributors never need Atlassian accounts, logins, or paid seat licenses. Mobile-friendly responsive upload portal with support for file sizes up to 1 GB per item.

How It Works: End-to-End Workflow

01
Create Form on Jira Issue: Open any Jira issue, select a template or add custom fields (files, text, dates, dropdowns), and set an optional due date.
02
Share Secure Link: Send the personalized link or have Collectly send a branded invitation email. The recipient accesses the form on mobile or desktop without an account.
03
Review & Approve in Jira: Submissions appear immediately on the Jira ticket. Review answers, approve valid items, or reject with a feedback note explaining what needs fixing.
04
Automated Chasing & Storage: Clients receive automatic reminders before due dates. Approved files flow to your chosen destination (Jira, S3, Azure, or GCS) seamlessly.

Built for High-Stakes Client Intake Teams

Collectly is purpose-built for teams that rely on external data to advance internal Jira workflows:

  • Accounting & Auditing: Collect tax returns, bank statements, receipts, and year-end audit confirmations.
  • Lending & Mortgage: Request W-2s, pay stubs, photo IDs, and credit authorizations with itemized reviews.
  • Legal & Compliance: Gather NDAs, signed contracts, evidentiary disclosures, and KYC documentation.
  • HR & Client Onboarding: Streamline contractor onboarding questionnaires, payroll setups, and certifications.

Quick Installation Guide

  1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
  2. Go to Apps → Explore new apps.
  3. Search for: Collectly: Client Forms & Document Requests for Jira.
  4. Click Get app and start on the free plan, or see paid plans.
  5. Open any issue and start requesting client forms and documents immediately!

Collectly: External File Collector & Client Uploads

Collect files, assets, and documents from external clients directly into Jira issues via secure, login-free links. No Atlassian accounts required for external clients.

Get Free on Atlassian Marketplace Knowledge Base → Standalone Cloud Platform

Eliminate Friction from Client Document Collection

Chasing clients, contractors, and external vendors for files is one of the biggest bottlenecks in project delivery. Instead of messy email attachment threads, broken Google Drive permissions, or purchasing expensive Atlassian licenses for third parties, Collectly gives your team a native 1-click upload link generator directly inside Jira issues.

1. Frictionless Client Experience

Clients and external partners upload documents through a clean, branded upload page with zero login or password required. Fully mobile-responsive drag-and-drop.

2. Direct Jira Attachments Sync

Uploaded files bypass your email inbox and are pushed straight into the target Jira issue's attachments list, accompanied by an internal audit confirmation comment.

3. Granular Security & Control

Configure upload links with custom expiration dates (hours or days), restrict allowed file formats/MIME types (PDF, images, zip), and instantly revoke link access at any moment.

4. High Capacity File Transfers

Designed to handle large files, creative briefs, design assets, and multi-file batches smoothly without hitting Jira instance upload hiccups.

5. Zero Licensing Costs

External contributors do not need Atlassian accounts or Jira seat licenses. The app itself is completely free for all team sizes on Jira Cloud.

6. Native Atlassian Forge Architecture

Built natively on Atlassian Forge for enterprise stability, seamless Jira issue panel integration, and compliant data handling.

How It Works in 4 Simple Steps

01
Generate Link: Open any Jira issue and click "Create Upload Link" inside the Collectly issue panel.
02
Configure Settings: Set optional expiration date, upload instructions, and allowed file formats.
03
Client Uploads: Send the link to your client via Slack, email, or WhatsApp. They drop files without logging in.
04
Instant Jira Attachment: Files appear immediately in the Jira ticket and an internal note confirms the upload.

Quick Installation Guide

  1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
  2. Go to Apps → Explore new apps.
  3. Search for: Collectly: External File Collector & Client Uploads.
  4. Click Get app (instant activation, 100% free).
  5. Open any issue and start requesting files right away!

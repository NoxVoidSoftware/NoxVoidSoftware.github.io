Privacy & Cookie Policy

This policy explains how NoxVoid Software ("NoxVoid", "we", "us") handles personal data when you visit noxvoidsoftware.github.io (the "Website") and how we use cookies. Our apps have their own policies: Collectly for Jira and CyclePulse.

1. Who is responsible

NoxVoid Software is the data controller for personal data processed through the Website under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: support@collectly.cloud.

2. What we collect and why

Contact form

When you send us a message, we receive your name, email address, the product you selected, and your message. The form is processed by a Cloudflare Worker and delivered to our team. We use this data only to answer your inquiry.

  • Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • Retention: as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any follow-up, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires otherwise.

Hosting and server logs

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Analytics and marketing (only with your consent)

We use Google Tag Manager to manage measurement tags, such as Google Analytics. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, these tags set cookies only if you allow them in the cookie banner; until you decide, Google Consent Mode keeps analytics and advertising storage switched off. Visitors elsewhere can opt out at any time.

  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw it at any time via .

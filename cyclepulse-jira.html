Actionable Delivery Metrics

CyclePulse gives engineering managers, Scrum masters, and agile teams total visibility into where Jira issues linger. By tracking time in each status and assignee on customizable working calendars, you eliminate guesswork from retrospectives and surface delays before sprint deadlines slip.

⏱️

See where work gets stuck

Time in every status with average, median and P85. The slowest status is flagged, rework visits are counted, and one click shows the issues behind any number. Flow Efficiency separates active work from waiting.

🗓️

Count real working hours, not calendar days

Set working days, hours and time zone. Add holidays, per-day shifts and overnight schedules. Share one calendar with the whole site, so every report and Jira field runs on the same clock.

🔎

Lead & Cycle Time as Jira fields

Two number fields the app keeps up to date. Filter with JQL "CyclePulse Lead Time (h)" > 48, show them on board cards and dashboards, and alert on slow work with Jira Automation. Backfill existing issues in one click.

Features

1. Time in Status Reports

Precise time in every status with Average, Median, and P85 percentile metrics. Automatically flags the slowest status, counts rework visits, and provides 1-click drill-downs into underlying issues.

2. Real Working Hours, Not Days

Count only true working hours. Configure business schedules, time zones, statutory holidays, weekend exclusions, and per-day shifts across a shared site-wide calendar.

3. Native Jira Fields & JQL

Adds fields like CyclePulse Lead Time (h) directly to Jira. Filter issues with JQL, display them on agile boards, and trigger Jira Automation rules.

4. Flow Efficiency Analytics

Measure the ratio of active development work versus waiting time (code review, blocked, QA queues), both per issue and across your entire project.

5. Assignee & Unassigned Duration

See how long work stays with individual assignees or sits idle in unassigned state to balance team workloads and prevent burnout.

6. Background Excel & CSV Exports

Export large datasets to .xlsx and .csv with real-time background progress tracking without freezing your browser.

How It Works

01
Set your working calendar: Choose working days, hours, time zone and holidays once for the whole site.
02
Pick a project or JQL: Open CyclePulse, select a project, board or filter, and the status history is read straight from Jira.
03
Find the bottleneck: Compare Average, Median and P85 per status, check Flow Efficiency, and drill into the issues behind any number.
04
Act on it in Jira: Use the Lead & Cycle Time fields in JQL, dashboards and Automation, or export the data to Excel or CSV.

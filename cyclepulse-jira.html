CyclePulse: Time in Status, Lead & Cycle Time Exports

Overview: Actionable Delivery Metrics

CyclePulse gives engineering managers, Scrum masters, and agile teams total visibility into where Jira issues linger. By tracking time in each status and assignee on customizable working calendars, you eliminate guesswork from retrospectives and surface delays before sprint deadlines slip.

1. See Where Work Gets Stuck

Precise time in every status with Average, Median, and P85 percentile metrics. Automatically flags the slowest status, counts rework visits, and provides 1-click drill-downs into underlying issues.

2. Real Working Hours, Not Days

Count only true working hours. Configure business schedules, time zones, statutory holidays, weekend exclusions, and per-day shifts across a shared site-wide calendar.

3. Native Jira Fields & JQL

Injects persistent fields like CyclePulse Lead Time (h) directly into Jira. Filter issues with JQL (e.g. "CyclePulse Lead Time (h)" > 48), display on agile boards, and trigger Jira Automation rules.

4. Flow Efficiency Analytics

Measure the ratio of active development work versus waiting time (code review, blocked, QA queues), both per issue and across your entire project.

5. Assignee & Unassigned Duration

See how long work stays with individual assignees or sits idle in unassigned state to balance team workloads and prevent burnout.

6. Background Excel & CSV Exports

Export massive datasets to .xlsx and .csv with real-time background progress tracking without freezing your browser.

## The App

The App maps customer email domains to Jira Service Management organizations, adds customers to those organizations when they raise requests, flags requests from public email providers and unknown domains, syncs existing users into organizations, and keeps an audit log of its routing decisions. The App runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. We may improve, change, or remove features over time.

The built-in list of public email providers is extensive but may not include every provider. We work to keep routing accurate, but you should review the audit log and the `unmapped-domain` and quarantine labels regularly.

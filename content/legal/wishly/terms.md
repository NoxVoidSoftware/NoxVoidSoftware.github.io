## The App

The App publishes the Jira Product Discovery ideas you choose to a Portal, lets visitors vote, suggest ideas, and subscribe to updates, writes vote counts into your Jira Product Discovery fields, and lets your team moderate suggestions in Jira. The Jira part runs on Atlassian Forge; Portals are hosted on Cloudflare. We may improve, change, or remove features over time.

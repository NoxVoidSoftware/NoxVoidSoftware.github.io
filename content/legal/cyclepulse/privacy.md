## Introduction & scope

NoxVoid Software ("NoxVoid", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how **CyclePulse: Time in Status, Lead & Cycle Time Exports** (the "App"), an Atlassian Forge app available on the Atlassian Marketplace, handles data from your Jira Cloud site.

## Runs on Atlassian — your data stays in Atlassian Cloud

CyclePulse is built entirely on the Atlassian Forge platform and carries the **Runs on Atlassian** badge. The App runs inside Atlassian's infrastructure, has no external server egress, and uses no external databases. NoxVoid does not receive, copy, or store your Jira issue data on any systems of its own.

## Data the App processes

To calculate Time in Status, Lead Time, Cycle Time, Flow Efficiency, and Assignee Time, the App reads from your Jira site:

- work item (issue) keys, fields, and status change history;
- assignee change history (who held a work item and for how long), shown with Jira user display names;
- the projects and filters you choose to analyze;
- Jira groups, used to control who can share and edit calendars and run exports.

The App stores its settings and work calendars (working hours, time zones, holidays, shifts) in Atlassian's Forge storage for your Jira site. It also writes Lead Time and Cycle Time values into its own Jira number fields so you can use them in JQL, boards, dashboards, and Automation. Excel and CSV exports are generated for and downloaded by the user who requested them.

## How we use data

Data is processed only to provide the App's features within your Jira site. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## Subprocessors

- **Atlassian** — Forge platform hosting, compute, and storage. Data is handled under [Atlassian's Privacy Policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy) and data residency settings for your site.

The App uses no other subprocessors.

## Data retention & deletion

App data lives in Forge storage for your Jira site. When the App is uninstalled, that data is removed by Atlassian under its Forge retention rules.

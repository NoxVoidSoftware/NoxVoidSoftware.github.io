How to use ClientStatus for monday.com

ClientStatus gives each of your clients a private, branded page with the status of their own tasks from your monday.com board. Clients don't need a monday.com account, and they only see what is theirs.

Before you start

  • A monday.com board with your client work, with a Status column. The status labels are what your client sees.
  • Optional: a column that names each task's client (text, dropdown or status), a date column for due dates, and email or phone columns for client contacts.
  • Installing the app needs a monday.com admin, or a member if your account lets members install apps.

1. Install and authorize

  1. Install ClientStatus from the monday.com apps marketplace.
  2. On your board, click + next to the board views and add the ClientStatus view.
  3. Click Authorize ClientStatus. A small window opens; approve access and it closes by itself. There is no separate sign-up: your ClientStatus workspace is created for your monday.com account.

2. Pick the columns

Tell ClientStatus who the client is:

  • One client: the whole board belongs to one client, who gets one link with every task.
  • A client per task: a column names each task's client; every client gets a link with only their tasks.
  • Decide later: every task gets its own link, and you can link clients on the ClientStatus website.

Then choose the Status column, and optionally the due date and note columns. Click Save: the items are imported, and from then on changes in monday.com show on the client pages within seconds.

3. Send each client their link

The board view lists your clients with their links. Click Copy link and send it to the client the way you usually do (email, chat). ClientStatus never emails your clients itself.

Every item also has its own link: open an item and the ClientStatus link tab in the item view.

What your client sees

A clean page with your workspace's name and logo, the progress of each task, its status and due date, and a place to comment. Comments arrive in monday.com as item updates.

Tips

  • Write #client in an item's update to show it to the client. Other updates stay internal.
  • Change a status, due date or client in monday.com or on the ClientStatus website: both stay in sync.
  • Sync now imports everything again if you changed many items at once.
  • Manage on ClientStatus opens the full website, where you set your logo and colors, manage clients and your team.
  • Users with view-only access in monday.com can copy and open client links; board members change the settings.

Plans

The free plan covers up to 10 active tasks. Closed tasks are always shown. To add more, choose a plan with Upgrade in the app; billing is handled by monday.com.

Uninstalling

When you uninstall the app, the connection to monday.com is removed at once and your client links keep working. If you don't install it again within 10 days, the data imported from your boards is deleted.

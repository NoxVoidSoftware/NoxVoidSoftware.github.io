Document Collection Built for Professionals

Accountants, mortgage brokers, legal counsel, and agencies waste countless hours chasing clients over scattered email threads, expired Dropbox links, and missing attachments. Collectly replaces the chaos with a single structured request link that clients actually complete.

1. Frictionless Client Portal

Clients open your unique tokenized link on phone or desktop and upload files directly. Zero login, zero passwords, zero accounts.

2. Dynamic Checklists & Templates

Build requests with required & optional items, file uploads or text answers. Includes pre-built templates for accounting, mortgage applications, legal intake, and onboarding.

3. Automated Nudge Reminders

Polite automated emails are dispatched 7, 3, and 1 days before the due date so your staff stops manually sending awkward follow-up emails.

4. 1-Click Structured ZIP Export

Once the client checklist hits 100%, download a clean, structured ZIP archive containing every uploaded document and text response organized by item.

5. Inline Document Previews

Inspect submitted PDFs, IDs, scans, and spreadsheets inline right in your browser without downloading every individual file first.

6. Custom Firm Branding

Present requests under your own brand with custom firm logos and isolated multi-tenant workspaces.

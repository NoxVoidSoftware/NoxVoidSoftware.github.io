DomainGuard: Customer Domain Router for JSM

Put every Jira Service Management customer in the right organization automatically, based on their email domain. Block Gmail-style public domains, flag unknown domains, and bring existing customers in with one click.

Overview: The Right Customer in the Right Organization

In Jira Service Management, organizations decide which customers can see each other's requests. Keeping them up to date by hand is slow and error-prone, and one mistake can show a customer another company's tickets. DomainGuard does it for you: when a request comes in, it reads the reporter's email domain and adds them to the organization you mapped to that domain.

1. Domain Mapping Matrix

Map up to 500 email domains to each JSM organization. Optionally match subdomains, limit a rule to specific service desks, and set a priority for requests from that customer.

2. Automatic Routing

Every new request is routed in the background: the reporter joins the right organization and the request's Organizations field is set. Auto-sharing and linking the organization to the service desk are opt-in per rule.

3. Freemail Shield

About 30,000 public email providers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and more) can never be mapped, so unrelated people are never grouped together. Add your own domains or exceptions; flagged requests get a quarantine-freemail label.

4. Tenant Isolation for Unknown Domains

Requests from domains you haven't mapped are tagged unmapped-domain, or fully isolated with quarantine-unmapped and an empty Organizations field until an agent reviews them.

5. Sync Existing Customers

Added a new rule? One click scans your existing users in the background and adds everyone with a matching domain to the organization, with live progress.

6. Audit Log & Retry

Every routing decision is logged for 30 days with masked email addresses. Filter by request key or date and retry any request in one click after fixing a rule.

🛡️

100% Forge-Native Architecture

DomainGuard is eligible for the Runs on Atlassian program. Built exclusively on the Atlassian Forge platform, your customers' data never leaves Atlassian Cloud — zero external server egress, zero external databases.

Quickstart (Under 5 Minutes)

  1. Install DomainGuard on your Jira Service Management Cloud site as an administrator.
  2. Open Jira Settings → Apps → DomainGuard Settings.
  3. Add a domain rule, for example acme.com → the Acme organization.
  4. Click Sync Existing Users to bring in customers you already have.
  5. Raise a test request from an @acme.com address and check the Audit Log.

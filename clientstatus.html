The Frictionless Client Communication Layer

Clients hate creating accounts or learning heavy project management software just to check on a deliverable. At the same time, giving them raw access to internal spreadsheets exposes sensitive margins, formulas, and other client data. ClientStatus bridges this gap with beautiful, branded, tokenized client portals updated directly from your Google Sheet.

1. Live Google Sheets 2-Way Sync

Connect your spreadsheet with Google OAuth. Update rows in your sheet internally — status percentages, notes, and milestones update on the client portal in real time.

2. Zero Client Logins

Clients never have to sign up, remember passwords, or download apps. Each project gets a secure, private tokenized URL (e.g. clientstatus.cloud/c/{token}).

3. Milestone Progress Timelines

Visual progress bars (e.g. 60% Complete) and custom pipeline stages (Planning → Design → Development → QA → Delivered).

4. Deliverable File Sharing

Attach contracts, PDFs, mockups, and final exports directly to client portals with 1-click downloads up to 100 GB.

5. Timestamped Update Notes

Leave quick progress notes (e.g. "Wireframes approved. Moving to high-fidelity design phase") without typing repetitive individual client emails.

6. White-Label Branding

Custom logo, custom brand color themes, and total removal of platform branding on Pro and Enterprise tiers for agencies.
